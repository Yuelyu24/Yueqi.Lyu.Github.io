01 / Research

Phishing

ClassificationEvaluation

The question

How can we evaluate an email classifier reliably?

I have worked on a research manuscript about phishing detection, focusing on data preparation, feature construction and classification models.

  • Consider how text, addresses and URLs can describe an email.
  • Keep training decisions separate from final test evaluation.
  • Use precision, recall and F1 to discuss different kinds of classification errors.

My main interest is in reproducible experiments and understanding why a model's performance changes.
